I've been writing here since 2016. It started as a weekly diary of trying to
bootstrap a company in Portugal, and drifted through WebGL, web components,
WebGPU, and tiny languages. Lately it's about what making software feels like
now that machines make it too. That's a lot of ground for a first visit, so
this page is the map.

## Software in the age of AI

The recent essays, and how most people end up here.

- [The end of creativity](/posts/the-end-of-creativity/): two groups of
  friends, two AI wedding videos, one identical result. A short story about
  regression to the mean.
- [Lost in the middle](/posts/lost-in-the-middle/): the post where I admitted
  my strongest skill had gone fragile, and decided this blog would change.
- [R.I.P. Rest In Prompt](/posts/rip-rest-in-prompt/): designing software
  architecture for machine collaboration instead of human readers.
- [I was banned from Claude for scaffolding a CLAUDE.md file](/posts/claude-code-banned-me/):
  exactly what the title says.

## Graphics programming

Where I spend most of my time: WebGPU, WGSL, and color.

- [WebGPU shader limits](/posts/webgpu-shader-limits/) and
  [the limits you won't find in the spec](/posts/webgpu-shader-unknown-limits/):
  an empirical hunt for the boundaries nobody wrote down.
- [WebGPU and the price of compiling WGSL](/posts/webgpu-diagnostics/): what
  shader compilation actually costs, measured with a tool you can run in your
  own browser.
- [Color picking Oklch for mortals](/posts/color-picking-oklch/): perceptual
  color without abandoning the classic color picker.
- [Infinite grid with just one square](/posts/infinite-grid-with-just-one-square/):
  a WebGL 2 instancing trick I still like.

## Small languages and tools

I like building tools for creative code, and lately everything is
s-expressions.

- [S-expressions the wrong way](/posts/sjon/): the detours that led me to
  SJON, my s-expression data format.
- [S-Rausch](/posts/s-rausch/): the demoscene DSL behind Farbrausch's
  Masagin, and the start of that obsession.
- [PNGine](/posts/introducing-pngine/): WebGPU shaders and pipelines bundled
  into a PNG file.
- [Introducing miniray](/posts/miniray/): a WGSL minifier born from a
  demoparty disaster.
- [The Zen of index.html](/posts/using-just-an-index-to-develop-a-web-app/):
  the case for developing a web app with nothing but one file.
- [The life of a Web Component](/posts/the-life-of-a-web-component-series/):
  a five-part series on the uncommon corners of web components.

## The bootstrapping years

Before all of that, this blog was a diary of building a design tool in
Portugal with no money, from 2016 to 2018.

- [Bootstrapping, week 1](/posts/bootstrapping1/): where it starts, hopeful.
- [Meeting the President](/posts/meeting-the-president/): an unexpected
  detour through Portuguese startup-land.
- [Eviction and Debt](/posts/eviction-and-debt/): where it bottoms out. The
  most honest thing on this site.
- The tool survived: [Grid Generator](/pages/gridgenerator/) is still up, and
